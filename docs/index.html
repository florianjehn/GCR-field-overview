Global catastrophic risk research

How is the field doing?

Research on global catastrophes grew a lot over the last decade. But most of the money now goes to AI, and the classic topics (nuclear war, volcanoes, space weather, food in a catastrophe) are running on fumes. This site collects the numbers behind that: who funds the work, how many people do it and how much gets published. Everything links back to its source, so you can check it.

How this was made

Grants were classified by topic and kind of work with a mix of the funders' own labels, rules and hand coding, and every unclear case went to a human decision. Each grant records who coded it and why. The full method, the decision log and a sensitivity analysis (how much the numbers move if you decide differently) are in the repository.

The caveats in short: only four philanthropic funders, no government money. LTFF and FLI publish only part of their grants. SFF figures are recommendations. Team pages are not payrolls. Amounts are nominal USD.

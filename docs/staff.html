How many people work on this?

This is not a count of everyone working on catastrophic risks. I only counted 16 organizations that I see as the core of the GCR and existential risk field. They are research centres (FHI, CSER, GCRI, ALLFED, BERI, LawAI, ALTER), policy groups (Global Shield, FLI, CLTR, Simon Institute, Good Ancestors, ERO) and teaching programmes (XLab, ERA, FernUni Hagen).

The much bigger AI safety and biosecurity fields are left out on purpose. AI labs' safety teams, MIRI, Redwood or the Johns Hopkins Center for Health Security aren't counted. So "mainly AI or bio" below only means GCR organizations that mostly ended up working on AI or bio, like FLI or CLTR, not the AI or bio fields as a whole.

For each organization I counted the people on its team page, using one archived snapshot per year from the Wayback Machine (the one closest to 1 July). The default view adds them up by what people do (research, teaching, policy or operations). Everything here is based on public data.

How I counted

A team page is not a payroll. Pages list part-timers and sometimes volunteers, and they change layout over time, so a jump can be a redesign rather than a hire. They are also often out of date. People who left stay listed for a while, and new hires show up late, so a count can be too high or too low. I count staff and paid research fellows, and leave out boards, advisors, affiliates, students and alumni. Who counts as what comes from the page's own section names and job titles.

What people do is coded per person. Most people get the type of their organization (research, policy or teaching). Admin and operations titles count as operations. Teaching posts at research organizations count as teaching, like the assistant professors for CSER's MPhil in Global Risk and Resilience. For CLTR and the FernUni programme there's no usable team page, so I use headcounts from their filings or website instead.

The totals only include organizations whose pages I could read in that year, so early years undercount. The list under the chart shows which organizations count in which years. If I knew that someone listed had already left, I took them out by hand.

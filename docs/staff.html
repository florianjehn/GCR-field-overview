A team page is not a payroll. Pages list part-timers, sometimes volunteers, and change layout over time, so a jump can be a redesign rather than a hire. Roles come from the page's own section names and job titles (rules in team_role_rules.csv). Organizations use the same y-axis so sizes are comparable.

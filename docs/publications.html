Is anyone still writing about this?

These are works in OpenAlex that use the exact phrase "global catastrophic risk". I count them two ways: with the phrase in the title or abstract (works that are actually about it), and with it anywhere in the text (which also catches passing mentions). The default shows works that mention nuclear war, volcanoes, asteroids, space weather or food, the topics I group as nuclear, natural & resilience.

This only finds the term, not the topic. Papers that talk about "existential risk", "catastrophic risk" or "societal collapse" instead aren't counted, and neither are the many papers on nuclear winter or pandemics that never use the phrase. So read this as how often people use the label, not as the size of the research field. Everything here is based on public data.

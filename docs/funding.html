What the topics mean

GCR outside AI and bio is the first three topics below together: abrupt sunlight reduction & infrastructure collapse, general x-risk, and climate & geoengineering. It only counts work framed as global catastrophic risk (so no mainstream climate funding).

Abrupt sunlight reduction & infrastructure collapse
Catastrophes that would block out the sun or knock out infrastructure over large areas, like nuclear war, large volcanic eruptions, asteroid impacts and severe space weather, and keeping food systems and infrastructure running through them.
AI
AI safety, alignment and AI governance.
Bio
Biosecurity and pandemic preparedness.
General x-risk
Catastrophic or existential risk as a whole, without a single hazard. For example multi-hazard institutes (FHI, GCRI), all-hazards policy (Global Shield) and student or fellowship programmes on x-risk (SERI, ERA, CERI).
Climate & geoengineering
Extreme climate change and research or governance of geoengineering.
Forecasting
Generic forecasting platforms and research (Metaculus, the Forecasting Research Institute and similar).
EA & community
Effective altruism community building and infrastructure.
Other
Grants I couldn't place from the public description and grants that are not about catastrophic risk at all.

All amounts are nominal USD. SFF figures are recommendations, not confirmed payouts, and include matching pledges (money SFF pays only if the recipient raises the same amount elsewhere). LTFF and FLI publish only part of their grants, so their totals are too low. 2026* runs only to November. Money passed on between these funders (regrants) is counted once. The data and classification are on GitHub.
